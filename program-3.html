Program 3: Accounting Vouchers

Contra, Payment, Receipt, Journal, Sales, Credit Note, Debit Note and Purchase vouchers.

Tally PrimeIntermediateAbout 60 min
After this program you will be able to:
  1. Select the correct voucher type for any transaction.
  2. Record Contra, Payment, Receipt, Journal, Sales, Credit Note, Debit Note and Purchase vouchers.
  3. Use bill-wise (reference) details for credit transactions.
  4. Verify the books with the Day Book, Cash Book, Bank Book, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss account and Balance Sheet.
Rule for this program. Pass every entry only through the voucher type named in its step. Use the Journal voucher only for transactions 14 and 15, which involve neither cash, bank nor a purchase or sale.

Choosing the voucher type

Voucher Key Use when Transactions
Contra F4 Money moves between cash and bank 2
Payment F5 Money goes out of cash or bank 3, 4, 10, 12, 13
Receipt F6 Money comes in to cash or bank 1, 9
Journal F7 No cash or bank involved (adjustments) 14, 15
Sales F8 Goods are sold 6, 11
Purchase F9 Goods are bought 5
Credit Note Ctrl + F8 Sales return 8
Debit Note Ctrl + F9 Purchase return 7
Case study: Mayura Traders (tap to open)

Mayura Traders, Chennai, is a trading firm owned by Mr. Ramesh. Its financial year is 01-04-2026 to 31-03-2027. Transactions for April 2026:

No. Date Transaction
1 01-Apr-2026 Mr. Ramesh started the business with capital of ₹5,00,000, deposited in HDFC Bank.
2 02-Apr-2026 Withdrew ₹50,000 cash from the bank for office use.
3 03-Apr-2026 Paid shop rent ₹15,000 in cash.
4 04-Apr-2026 Bought furniture for ₹60,000, paid by cheque.
5 05-Apr-2026 Purchased goods on credit from Kumar Suppliers ₹2,00,000.
6 08-Apr-2026 Sold goods on credit to Anand Stores ₹2,50,000.
7 10-Apr-2026 Returned goods worth ₹10,000 to Kumar Suppliers.
8 12-Apr-2026 Anand Stores returned goods worth ₹8,000.
9 15-Apr-2026 Received ₹1,00,000 from Anand Stores by cheque, deposited in the bank.
10 18-Apr-2026 Paid Kumar Suppliers ₹1,20,000 through the bank.
11 22-Apr-2026 Cash sales ₹50,000.
12 25-Apr-2026 Paid salary ₹25,000 in cash.
13 27-Apr-2026 Mr. Ramesh withdrew ₹10,000 from the bank for personal use.
14 28-Apr-2026 Electricity charges of ₹3,500 for April are outstanding.
15 30-Apr-2026 Provide depreciation on furniture at 10% p.a.

Before you start

  1. At the Gateway press F2 (Period) and set From 01-04-2026, To 30-04-2026.
  2. For each voucher press the key named in the step, then F2 to set the date.
  3. Accept every voucher with Ctrl + A.

In Payment, Receipt and Contra vouchers the first line is Account (the cash or bank ledger); the other ledger goes under Particulars. Tally chooses Dr or Cr for you.

Steps

1Transaction 1: Receipt voucher (capital introduced)
  • Voucher / key / date: Receipt, F6, 01-04-2026.
  • Account: HDFC Bank A/c. Particulars: Capital A/c. Amount: 5,00,000.
  • Narration: Capital introduced by Mr. Ramesh.
  • Journal entry: HDFC Bank A/c Dr 5,00,000; To Capital A/c 5,00,000.
2Transaction 2: Contra voucher (cash withdrawn from bank)
  • Voucher / key / date: Contra, F4, 02-04-2026.
  • Debit: Cash 50,000. Credit: HDFC Bank A/c 50,000.
  • Journal entry: Cash A/c Dr 50,000; To HDFC Bank A/c 50,000.

Contra is the only voucher where both sides are cash or bank. Tally will not offer an expense ledger here.

3Transaction 3: Payment voucher (rent paid in cash)
  • Voucher / key / date: Payment, F5, 03-04-2026.
  • Account: Cash. Particulars: Rent. Amount: 15,000.
  • Journal entry: Rent A/c Dr 15,000; To Cash A/c 15,000.
4Transaction 4: Payment voucher (furniture by cheque)
  • Voucher / key / date: Payment, F5, 04-04-2026.
  • Account: HDFC Bank A/c. Particulars: Furniture. Amount: 60,000.
  • In the bank details set Transaction type = Cheque and enter a cheque number (for example 100001).
  • Journal entry: Furniture A/c Dr 60,000; To HDFC Bank A/c 60,000.
5Transaction 5: Purchase voucher (credit purchase)
  1. Press F9 (Purchase), date 05-04-2026. Use Accounting Invoice mode (Ctrl + H if Tally shows item invoice).
  2. Supplier invoice no. = KS/101, Date = 05-04-2026.
  3. Party A/c name: Kumar Suppliers. In Bill Allocations choose New Ref, Name = KS/101, Amount = 2,00,000.
  4. Ledger: Purchases, 2,00,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: Purchases A/c Dr 2,00,000; To Kumar Suppliers 2,00,000.
You should see: Kumar Suppliers with a pending bill of ₹2,00,000.
6Transaction 6: Sales voucher (credit sale)
  1. Press F8 (Sales), date 08-04-2026, Accounting Invoice mode.
  2. Party A/c name: Anand Stores. Keep the Invoice no. Tally proposes (for example 1).
  3. In Bill Allocations choose New Ref, Name = the invoice number, Amount = 2,50,000.
  4. Ledger: Sales, 2,50,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: Anand Stores Dr 2,50,000; To Sales 2,50,000.
7Transaction 7: Debit Note voucher (purchase return)
  1. Press Ctrl + F9 (Debit Note), date 10-04-2026.
  2. Party A/c name: Kumar Suppliers. In Bill Allocations choose Agst Ref, select KS/101 and adjust 10,000.
  3. Ledger: Purchase Return, 10,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: Kumar Suppliers Dr 10,000; To Purchase Return 10,000.
8Transaction 8: Credit Note voucher (sales return)
  1. Press Ctrl + F8 (Credit Note), date 12-04-2026.
  2. Party A/c name: Anand Stores. In Bill Allocations choose Agst Ref, select the sales invoice and adjust 8,000.
  3. Ledger: Sales Return, 8,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: Sales Return Dr 8,000; To Anand Stores 8,000.
9Transaction 9: Receipt voucher (cheque from customer)
  1. Press F6 (Receipt), date 15-04-2026.
  2. Account: HDFC Bank A/c. Particulars: Anand Stores, 1,00,000.
  3. In Bill Allocations choose Agst Ref, select the sales invoice and adjust 1,00,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: HDFC Bank A/c Dr 1,00,000; To Anand Stores 1,00,000.
You should see: Anand Stores’ pending bill reduced to ₹1,42,000 (2,50,000 - 8,000 - 1,00,000).
10Transaction 10: Payment voucher (supplier paid by bank)
  1. Press F5 (Payment), date 18-04-2026.
  2. Account: HDFC Bank A/c. Particulars: Kumar Suppliers, 1,20,000.
  3. In Bill Allocations choose Agst Ref, select KS/101 and adjust 1,20,000. Accept with Ctrl + A.
  • Journal entry: Kumar Suppliers Dr 1,20,000; To HDFC Bank A/c 1,20,000.
You should see: Kumar Suppliers’ pending bill reduced to ₹70,000 (2,00,000 - 10,000 - 1,20,000).
11Transaction 11: Sales voucher (cash sale)
  • Voucher / key / date: Sales, F8, 22-04-2026.
  • Party A/c name: Cash. Ledger: Sales, 50,000.
  • Journal entry: Cash Dr 50,000; To Sales 50,000.
12Transaction 12: Payment voucher (salary)
  • Voucher / key / date: Payment, F5, 25-04-2026.
  • Account: Cash. Particulars: Salary, 25,000.
  • Journal entry: Salary Dr 25,000; To Cash 25,000.
13Transaction 13: Payment voucher (drawings)
  • Voucher / key / date: Payment, F5, 27-04-2026.
  • Account: HDFC Bank A/c. Particulars: Drawings, 10,000.
  • Journal entry: Drawings Dr 10,000; To HDFC Bank A/c 10,000.
14Transaction 14: Journal voucher (outstanding expense)
  • Voucher / key / date: Journal, F7, 28-04-2026.
  • Line 1: Dr Electricity Charges 3,500. Line 2: Cr Outstanding Expenses 3,500.
  • Narration: Electricity charges for April outstanding.

In a Journal voucher you choose Dr or Cr on every line, and total Dr must equal total Cr.

15Transaction 15: Journal voucher (depreciation)
  • Voucher / key / date: Journal, F7, 30-04-2026.
  • Line 1: Dr Depreciation 6,000. Line 2: Cr Furniture 6,000.
  • Working: ₹60,000 x 10% = ₹6,000.
  • Narration: Depreciation on furniture at 10% p.a.
You should see: Furniture reduced to ₹54,000 (60,000 - 6,000).
16Test the deletion rule from Program 2
  1. Open Chart of Accounts -> Ledgers, select Rent and press Enter.
  2. Press Alt + D to delete.
You should see: Tally refuses, because vouchers are posted against Rent. You would have to delete or alter those vouchers first.

Troubleshooting

Problem Likely cause Fix
Cannot pick a bill in Agst Ref Party ledger lacks bill-by-bill Alter the ledger and set it to Yes
Ledger not offered in a voucher Wrong voucher type Use the voucher type named in the step
Trial Balance totals differ Wrong amount or ledger in a voucher Check the Day Book voucher by voucher
Date not accepted Period not set or date outside the year Press F2 and correct the date
Journal will not accept Dr and Cr not equal Correct the amounts until the difference is zero

Program 10: Preparation of Ratio Analysis

Opening balances, vouchers, closing stock and adjustments, then ratios, funds flow statement and cash flow statement.

Tally PrimeIntermediateAbout 90 min
After this program you will be able to:
  1. Prepare the books with opening balances, vouchers, closing stock and adjustments.
  2. View the Ratio Analysis and explain the current, quick, gross profit and net profit ratios.
  3. View the Funds Flow Statement.
  4. View the Cash Flow Statement and explain why a Contra entry does not appear in it.

Concepts in brief

  • Ratio analysis compares figures of the final accounts to judge liquidity and profitability.
  • Funds flow statement shows where funds came from and how they were used during the year.
  • Cash flow statement shows the movement of cash and bank balances.
  • Closing stock is shown on the credit side of the Trading Account and as an asset. It is entered, with the closing date, by altering the stock ledger.
  • Depreciation reduces an asset and is charged to the Profit & Loss Account.
  • Outstanding expense is added to the expense and shown as a liability; prepaid expense is deducted from the expense and shown as an asset.
  • Provision for doubtful debts is an estimated loss on debtors, charged to the Profit & Loss Account and deducted from debtors.
Rule. Every balance of the Trial Balance is entered as an opening balance. Each transaction is passed only through its own voucher type. Journal vouchers are used only for the adjustments, dated 31-03-2026. The closing stock is entered by altering the stock ledger, not by a voucher.

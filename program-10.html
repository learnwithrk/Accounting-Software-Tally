Concepts in brief

  • Closing stock is shown on the credit side of the Trading Account and as an asset.
  • Depreciation is a loss from use of an asset; it reduces the asset and is charged to the Profit & Loss Account.
  • Outstanding expense is an expense incurred but not yet paid; add it to the expense and show a liability.
  • Prepaid expense is paid in advance; deduct it from the expense and show an asset.
  • Provision for doubtful debts is an estimated loss on debtors, charged to the Profit & Loss Account and deducted from debtors.
Rule. Pass all five adjustments only through Journal vouchers dated 31-03-2027.

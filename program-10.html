Program 10: Adjustments and Final Accounts

Enter a Trial Balance as opening balances, make the year-end adjustments and prepare the final accounts.

Tally PrimeIntermediateAbout 75 min
After this program you will be able to:
  1. Create ledgers with opening balances, including a stock ledger under Stock-in-Hand.
  2. Show the closing stock by altering the stock ledger.
  3. Pass depreciation, outstanding expense, prepaid expense and provision adjustments through Journal vouchers.
  4. Display the Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet.

Concepts in brief

  • Closing stock is shown on the credit side of the Trading Account and as an asset. It is entered, with the closing date, by altering the stock ledger.
  • Depreciation is a loss from use of an asset; it reduces the asset and is charged to the Profit & Loss Account.
  • Outstanding expense is an expense incurred but not yet paid; add it to the expense and show a liability.
  • Prepaid expense is paid in advance; deduct it from the expense and show an asset.
  • Provision for doubtful debts is an estimated loss on debtors, charged to the Profit & Loss Account and deducted from debtors.
Rule. Every balance of the Trial Balance is entered as an opening balance. Journal vouchers are used only for the adjustments, dated 31-03-2027. The closing stock is entered by altering the stock ledger, not by a voucher.

7Adjustment 1: show the closing stock by altering the Stock ledger
  1. Alt + G -> Chart of Accounts -> Ledgers, select Stock (under Stock-in-Hand) and press Enter (Alter mode).
  2. Enter the closing date 31-03-2027 and the closing stock value of 35,000, then press Ctrl + A.

The opening stock of ₹40,000 stays as the opening balance of the ledger. The closing stock of ₹35,000 is shown on the credit side of the Trading Account and as an asset in the Balance Sheet. The label of the closing stock field can differ slightly between Tally releases.

You should see: Closing Stock of ₹35,000 in the Trading Account.

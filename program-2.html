Program 2: Chart of Accounts

Groups and ledgers: create (single and multiple), alter and delete.

Tally PrimeBeginnerAbout 40 min
After this program you will be able to:
  1. Explain pre-defined, primary and sub-groups.
  2. Create groups and ledgers (single and multiple).
  3. Alter and delete ledgers and groups, and explain when deletion is refused.

Concepts in brief

  • A group classifies ledgers; a ledger is the account in which transactions are recorded.
  • Tally has 28 pre-defined groups: 15 primary groups (for example Capital Account, Current Assets, Current Liabilities, Direct Expenses, Indirect Expenses, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts) and 13 sub-groups (for example Bank Accounts, Cash-in-Hand, Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors).
  • A primary group is at the top of the hierarchy; a sub-group sits under another group.
  • Transactions are posted only to ledgers, never to groups.

Steps

1Open the Chart of Accounts
  1. Press Alt + G, type Chart of Accounts and select it.
  2. Choose Groups to see the pre-defined groups.
You should see: groups with their parents. Bank Accounts sits under Current Assets.
2Create the sub-groups
  1. Alt + G -> Create Master -> Group.
  2. Enter the first sub-group, then press Ctrl + A:
Name Under
Staff Costs Indirect Expenses
Local Suppliers Sundry Creditors
  1. Repeat for the second row.
You should see: both names under their parent groups in the Chart of Accounts.
3Create the primary group
  1. Alt + G -> Create Master -> Group.
  2. Name = Marketing Expenses. For Under choose Primary.
  3. Set Nature of group = Expenses and Group behaves like a sub-ledger = No. Press Ctrl + A.
You should see: Marketing Expenses at the top level, beside Direct Expenses and Indirect Expenses.
4Create ledgers one at a time (single ledger)

Alt + G -> Create Master -> Ledger. Enter the Name, choose Under, leave the opening balance blank and press Ctrl + A. Repeat for each row.

Ledger Under Extra settings
Capital A/c Capital Account None
Cash Cash-in-Hand None
HDFC Bank A/c Bank Accounts Bank details if asked
Furniture Fixed Assets None
Purchases Purchase Accounts Inventory values are affected = No
Sales Sales Accounts Inventory values are affected = No
Purchase Return Purchase Accounts Inventory values are affected = No
Sales Return Sales Accounts Inventory values are affected = No
Kumar Suppliers Local Suppliers Maintain balances bill-by-bill = Yes; credit period 30 days
Anand Stores Sundry Debtors Maintain balances bill-by-bill = Yes; credit period 30 days

Switch on bill-by-bill only for party ledgers. It lets you match payments and returns to invoices in Program 3.

You should see: each ledger listed under its group.
5Create several ledgers at once (multiple ledgers)
  1. Alt + G -> Chart of Accounts -> Ledgers.
  2. Press Alt + H (Multi-Masters) -> Multi Create and press Enter.
  3. For each block choose Under, then type the names in the Name column.
Under Ledger names
Indirect Expenses Rent; Electricity Charges; Depreciation; Conveyance; Telephone Charges
Staff Costs Salary
Capital Account Drawings
Current Liabilities Outstanding Expenses
Marketing Expenses Advertisement
  1. Press Ctrl + A to accept.
You should see: all new ledgers under their groups, created in a single screen.
6Alter ledgers and groups

Task A: rename a ledger. Open Chart of Accounts -> Ledgers, select Conveyance, press Enter and change Name to Travelling Expenses. Save.

Task B: move a ledger. Alter Advertisement, change Under to Indirect Expenses, save. Alter again and change it back to Marketing Expenses.

Task C: rename a group. In Groups select Local Suppliers, press Enter and change Name to Trade Creditors - Local. Save.

Changing a ledger’s parent group changes where it appears in the Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss account. Take a backup before major changes.

You should see: Kumar Suppliers still sits under the renamed group.
7Delete a ledger
  1. Select Telephone Charges in the Chart of Accounts and press Enter (Alter mode).
  2. Press Alt + D (Delete) and confirm with Y.

A group can be deleted only after all its ledgers and sub-groups are deleted or moved.

You should see: Telephone Charges removed, because it has no transactions.
Checkpoint 2. Tick each item before moving on.

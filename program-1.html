Program 1: Fundamentals: Company Creation

Create, alter, shut, select and back up a company, and set its currency.

Tally PrimeBeginnerAbout 30 min
After this program you will be able to:
  1. Create a company with its name, address, e-mail and currency.
  2. Choose between Accounts Only and Accounts with Inventory.
  3. Select, alter, shut and back up a company.

Concepts in brief

  • A company is a separate set of books with its own data folder.
  • The financial year is the twelve-month accounting period. Books beginning from is the date from which you start entering vouchers.
  • Accounts Only keeps money records without stock details.

Steps

1Open the company creation screen
  1. Open Tally Prime. The Select Company screen appears (or the Gateway of Tally if a company is open).
  2. On the Gateway press Alt + K (Company) and choose Create. On the Select Company screen choose Create Company.
You should see: the Company Creation form with blank fields.
2Enter the company details

Fill each field and press Enter to move on.

Field Enter this
Directory Keep the default or choose your lab folder
Name Mayura Traders
Mailing name Mayura Traders
Address No. 45, Gandhi Road, T. Nagar, Chennai
Country India
State Tamil Nadu
Pincode 600017
Telephone / Mobile 98400 12345
E-mail accounts@mayuratraders.in
Financial year beginning from 01-04-2026
Books beginning from 01-04-2026

The financial year and books-beginning dates cannot be changed casually later. Check them before you accept.

3Set the currency and number format
  1. In the same form find the Base currency symbol group.
  2. Enter: Symbol = ₹; Formal name = INR (or Rupees); Suffix symbol to amount = No; Decimal places = 2; Word after decimal = paise.
  3. Press Ctrl + A and confirm Accept? Yes.

To view the currency later: Alt + G -> Alter Master -> Currency.

You should see: the Gateway of Tally with Mayura Traders named in the corner.
4Choose "Maintain Accounts Only"
  • In some releases the creation form asks Maintain: choose Accounts Only.
  • In other releases press F11 (Features) -> Inventory Features and set Integrate accounts with inventory to No.

Accounts Only suits a business that records money values only. The case study has no stock records, so we use it.

You should see: no stock items or godown prompts in the Gateway.
5Alter the company details
  1. Press Alt + K (Company) -> Alter and select Mayura Traders.
  2. Change E-mail to info@mayuratraders.in.
  3. Press Ctrl + A to save.
You should see: the new e-mail when you open the company details again.
6Create, shut and select companies
  1. Press Alt + K -> Create. Create Demo Traders (State = Tamil Nadu, dates 01-04-2026). Accept with Ctrl + A.
  2. Press Alt + F1 (Shut Company). If two companies are open, choose Demo Traders.
  3. Press F1 (Select Company), highlight Mayura Traders and press Enter.

Shut vs. delete. Shutting only closes a company and keeps its data. Deleting removes it permanently. In this lab, shut Demo Traders; do not delete it.

You should see: Mayura Traders open and Demo Traders closed.
7Take a backup
  1. Press Alt + Y (Data) -> Backup.
  2. Select the company, choose the Destination folder and confirm.

Restore later with Alt + Y -> Restore.

You should see: a message that the backup completed.
Checkpoint 1. Tick each item before moving on.
